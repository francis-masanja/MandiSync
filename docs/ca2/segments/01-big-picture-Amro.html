The Big Picture

MandiSync is one shared system for booking a mandi slot, checking the gate and recording weight. It replaces paper slips and phone calls with one live record.

Owner: Amro Area: Middleware & Security

In one minute

MandiSync brings the whole mandi visit into one place. A farmer can find a mandi, check the space left and book a one-hour arrival window. At the mandi, the gate is checked, the weight is recorded, and everyone sees the same queue status.

What this part covers

When a farmer opens MandiSync, they see nearby mandis, check the remaining capacity and book a one-hour arrival window. Once at the mandi, the gate desk scans the RFID tag or QR pass. The weighbridge records both gross and tare weights, and the queue ledger updates for everyone.

Mandis still rely on physical queues, handwritten registers and repeated phone calls, which is why the project exists. Farmers end up travelling without knowing the wait, and staff end up copying the same details into more than one place. MandiSync keeps a single record from booking all the way to completion.

Key terms

Mandi A regulated agricultural market where farmers bring produce to be weighed and sold.
Slot A one-hour arrival window that a farmer books in advance.
Gate pass A signed token or QR code that proves a booking is valid at the gate.
Telemetry A reading sent by a device, such as an RFID scan or a scale weight.
